Voiceless stops

A stop closes the tract completely and lets go with a burst. Only the place changes: lips (p), ridge (t), palate (c), soft palate (k), uvula (q). Slide to choose a place and let go to hear it.

Press the line and slide. Hold a symbol to hear exactly that one.
