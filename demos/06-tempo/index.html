Tempo

Faster is not the same as sped up.

Real speakers shorten vowels a lot and consonants a little, because a consonant has a physical job to do. Fast vowels also fall short of their targets. Compare the two ways of going fast: speech-like and naive, which squeezes the whole score evenly.

Wider = longer. Purple: vowels. Grey: consonants. Watch how the grey stays wide when the purple shrinks.
