Rhythm

Which clock does the language keep?

In a syllable-timed language every syllable takes about the same time. In a stress-timed language the stressed syllables are the beats, and the syllables between them are squeezed to fit, which is also why unstressed vowels shrink to ə. Real languages only lean one way. Here you choose.

Each box is a syllable, as wide as it lasts. Purple boxes are stressed. In syllable-timed speech the boxes are the same width; in stress-timed speech the purple ones recur at an even beat.
