Press and drag on the IPA chart. Hold to sound.

The chart is a map of the tongue: left is front, right is back, top is close to the roof of the mouth, bottom is open. Where two symbols share a spot, the right one has rounded lips. The dot is where your pointer is; on the right, the vocal tract takes the shape that position asks for.
