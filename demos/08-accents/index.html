Accents

An accent here is a short list of rewrite rules applied to the IPA before it is sounded. θ > s turns every θ into s; t > ʔ / V _ V does it between vowels only. Pick an accent, then edit its rules and hear the difference. These are sketches of tendencies, not portraits of real people.
